Prompt

Create an Early Renaissance fresco painting inspired by Giotto's Lamentation. Show a group of realistic human figures gathered around an important religious event. Use linear perspective to create the illusion of depth and space. Include natural-looking proportions, facial expressions, and body language that communicate emotion. Use soft colors, realistic light and shadow, and carefully arranged figures that lead the viewer's eye toward the focal point. Balance the composition with a believable landscape and architectural background. The painting should emphasize observation of the real world rather than the flat, symbolic style of medieval art.

Prompt

Create an Ancient Egyptian funerary mask inspired by the Funerary Mask of Tutankhamun. The mask should be made of gold with bright blue decorative details and precious gemstones. Use smooth lines, perfect symmetry, and an idealized youthful face to represent eternal life. Include traditional Egyptian symbols such as a cobra and vulture on the headdress to emphasize royal authority and divine protection. The composition should feel balanced and orderly, with rich color contrast between gold and blue. The polished surface should reflect light, creating a sense of importance and sacredness. The overall design should appear timeless, powerful, and connected to religious beliefs about life after death.
