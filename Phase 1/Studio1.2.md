**Assignment Title**: Visual Prompt Studio Phase 1.
 **Synthwave / Retro 1980s**

**Prompt**:Create a Synthwave/Retro 1980s album cover featuring a futuristic musician standing on a glowing neon grid beneath a bright pink, orange, and purple sunset. Chrome skyscrapers and palm trees fill the background, creating a retro-futuristic vibe. Value is shown through a bright neon-blue light behind the musician, while deep purple and black shadows create strong contrast and a dramatic mood. Create a sense of space by making the grid lines large in the foreground and smaller as they move toward the horizon. Overlapping palm trees and buildings, along with distant objects that fade into the background to help create depth. Show movement with glowing laser streaks across the sky and a speeding futuristic car leaving light trails behind it. Create unity by using the same neon color palette of pink, purple, turquoise, and orange throughout the design. Add variety with a large crystal rising from the grid as an unexpected shape and texture. Use asymmetrical balance by placing the oversized musician slightly off-center as the main focal point. Create rhythm through the repeating grid lines, laser beams, and neon colors that guide the viewer’s eye across the cover. The overall album cover should feel energetic, nostalgic, and cinematic while capturing the bold spirit of 1980s synthwave music. 

**Vocabulary Breakdown**
Value – Value is how light or dark something is in a piece of art. It helps create contrast and can make a picture feel bright, dramatic, or moody. Space – Space is the area around and between objects. Artists use space to make a flat image look like it has depth and distance. Unity – Unity is when all the parts of an artwork work well together. Things like repeating colors, shapes, or themes help create unity. Balance – Balance is how the visual weight is spread out in a design. It helps the artwork feel stable and not too crowded on one side. Rhythm – Rhythm is created by repeating colors, lines, shapes, or patterns. It helps move your eyes through the artwork and creates a sense of flow.
